- Access rating for ceilings. Table 5 lists work at height, including ceilings, as Extreme. Existing entries use Simple (Macquarie 16, 20) and Challenging (Macquarie 5, 10) for ceilings. The Macquarie fill follows Table 5 (Extreme). One convention should be applied across all stations.

Existing entries are mixed: Mawson 7 cold porch Challenging, Mawson 31 dado wall Challenging, Macquarie 17 and 19 Simple (unoccupied, acceptable), Davis 18 workshop Simple (occupied, should be Challenging).
- End-of-life wording. Column O uses ">2040", "Before 2040", a year, or blank. "Before 2040" rows at Mawson (51 to 53) were given 2037; others with "Before 2040" were left as No removal. The report asks that justification be recorded where removal is pushed past 10 years (3.2); rows 19, 26, 34 to 39 at Mawson carry "Needs demo" with no year and would benefit from a recorded reason.

## Consistency by item type across all four sheets

Every row on Casey, Davis, Mawson and Macquarie (existing and newly filled) was grouped by item type and the decision columns compared side by side. Items in the same group should carry the same requirement, treatment, season, reinstatement and adjuster pattern unless the commentary says why not.

### Internal wall linings (exposed, painted)
- Treatment and season are consistent: Preference, Removal - Full, Winter on Casey, Mawson and Macquarie. Davis 18 is Summer (Priority Assessed); acceptable for Davis access but it is the only internal lining in Summer.
- Reinstatement is consistent by item (Cold Porch Lining, Dado Wall, Plasterboard). Mawson 48 (behind tiles, medical suite) has no reinstatement option and no Z; add Wall Int. – Plasterboard.
- Complexity convention adopted: it reflects how hard the work is, not the quantity, and is rated by the material type in column V so rows of the same type carry the same rating (mixed wall-and-ceiling rows take the rating of their coded type). Internal wall linings, soffits, doors, gaskets and panels are Low; ceilings and roof-space work are Medium; underfloor, foundations, formwork and structural interfaces are High. Existing wall-lining rows at Medium or High (Casey 12, Davis 6, 18, Macquarie 7 is Low, Macquarie 10 High, Mawson 7, 51, 53) can stay if the commentary gives the reason for the extra difficulty; otherwise Low.
- Access: Davis 18 (occupied workshop) was Simple while Casey 12, Mawson 7, 31 and the Macquarie workshop are Challenging. Changed to Challenging in the filled copy at the user's request.

### Ceilings
- Access convention adopted: ceilings, roof spaces and soffits are Extreme (work at height). All newly filled Macquarie ceilings and soffits follow it. Macquarie 10 (Challenging), 16 and 20 (Simple) are the only ceiling removals that do not; change to Extreme.
- Complexity convention adopted: ceilings are Medium. Macquarie 10 is High; change to Medium unless the roof-space sandwich construction is the reason, in which case say so in U.
- End-of-life ceilings (Macquarie 16, 20) correctly have no reinstatement and are the season before demolition. Consistent with the walls in the same buildings.

### Floor sheeting and underfloor

- End-of-life floors are removed in the demolition season on all sheets (Casey 11, Macquarie 18, Mawson 52), consistent with the flooring rule.
- Access for underfloor end-of-life removal differs: Macquarie 18 Challenging, Mawson 52 Extreme. Subfloor work is a confined space, so Extreme on both.
