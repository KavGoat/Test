**Fill-ins.** Four buildings can be made completely ACM free by the end of the 10-year window: ANARESAT Equipment Building, Balloon Release Building, Receiver Building and the Red Shed (crawlway boards). The Transmitter Building is removed by demolition in 2037. The Main Power House and the Operations Building cannot be made completely ACM free without full demolition, because of their floor sheets and subfloor.

**Key findings:**

- Peak spend of $264k in 2032, driven by removal of the Main Power House dado wall lining during a planned shutdown.
- Unquantified ACM not in the program: Jack's Donga insulation, Mabel Hut floor, Robinson's Ridge Hut floor and debris around the old Carpenter's Workshop. All are huts or debris and are managed per AMP.

- The program is small ($0.67M, 105 labour days) and has no works in 2030 or from 2033 to 2036.
- The Red Shed crawlway is costed at the full 72 m2 although the register notes the main subfloor sheets were removed in 2025. The cost is likely overstated.
